ML Pipeline

From raw data to a production-ready model

The model goes through a rigorous pipeline of preprocessing, feature engineering, and evaluation before it's ever used to predict your car's value.

1

Data Ingestion & Cleaning

Load CarDekho CSV, drop duplicates, parse mileage/engine/power into numerics, remove outlier prices above ₹50L.

2

Feature Engineering

Car age calculated from year. Label encoding applied to fuel, transmission, seller type, owner, and brand.

3

Train / Test Split

80% training, 20% held-out test. All fuel types and price ranges represented proportionally.

4

Random Forest Training

100 decision trees trained in parallel. Each tree sees a random subset of features, preventing overfitting.

5

Model Serialization

Trained model saved as a .pkl file. Loaded once at server startup — predictions in milliseconds.

The Algorithm

What actually drives your car's price

Random Forest reveals which features matter most. Year and KMs driven dominate — brand perception, fuel costs, and power also play significant roles.

🌲
Ensemble Learning
100 trees vote on the prediction. The average of their outputs is the final price — far more stable than a single tree.
🛡️
Resistant to Overfitting
Each tree sees a random subset of features — no single factor dominates and the model generalizes well to unseen cars.
📊
